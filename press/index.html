Descriptions

Short (about 50 words)

Squeeze Out! is a satisfying jelly squish puzzle. Every block is real, wobbly soft-body jelly: slide each one to the gate of its color, and when a block is one cell too wide, squeeze it through with a bulge, a squelch and a pop. Free to play on iPhone, iPad, iPhone Duo and Android.

Long (about 230 words)

Squeeze Out! is a puzzle you can feel. Every block on the board is real soft-body jelly that wobbles, bulges and stretches under your finger. Slide each block to the gate of its color to clear the board. When a block is one cell too wide, a squeeze gate lets it squish through: it stretches its neck, bulges and pops out with a juicy splat you hear and feel in the haptics. Two cells too wide and it boings right back, so every board is a small plan of which jelly goes first and where to spend your limited squeezes.

Every few levels the frost cracks and frees a new jelly friend: fruit, desserts, gummy animals, sea creatures and stranger finds, each with its own wobble and voice. Twelve rules, from frozen gummies to taffy twins and gloop, keep the 120 launch levels across 6 chapters fresh, with new level packs downloading in the game and a Daily Jam every day.

Between puzzles, Free mode is a calm physics sandbox with no timer: poke, slap, stretch and pinch-to-resize your whole collection. And for fun, Jellify anything: turn a photo of your cat, dog or favorite thing into a jelly. The cutout runs on your phone and the photo is never uploaded.

On iPhone Duo, fold or unfold mid-level and the board re-fits itself live, without a restart.

Key features

  • Real soft-body jelly. Every block is simulated as a soft body, so it wobbles, bulges, stretches and splats instead of snapping into place.
  • Squeeze gates. A block one cell too wide can squish through a narrower gate, and each level gives you a limited number of squeezes.
  • ASMR sound and haptics. Squelches, plops and pops on every move, matched with haptic feedback.
  • Free a jelly friend. Every few levels the frost cracks and a new jelly joins your Jelly Book.
  • 120 levels, 6 chapters, 12 rules at launch, plus new level packs that download in the game and a Daily Jam remix every day.
  • Free mode. A timer-free sandbox to poke, slap, stretch and pinch-to-resize your jellies, with unlockable scenes like the Aquarium and zero-gravity Space.
  • Jellify anything. Turn a photo into a playable jelly. On iPhone the cutout uses Apple's Vision framework on the device; on Android it uses on-device ML Kit. Photos are never uploaded.
  • Plays offline. Levels, Free mode and the collection work without Wi-Fi. No account or login.

Built for iPhone Duo

Squeeze Out! has a wide layout for both iPhone Duo displays and switches between them live.

  • Live fold and unfold. Fold or unfold mid-level and the board re-fits itself without a restart. The clock keeps running, and a jelly you are dragging glides to the new board and finishes the move.
  • Wider boards. On the wider screens the board grows and the timer, squeeze counter and boosters merge into one bottom bar.
  • Wide menus. Home, the Jelly Book, the shop and the level map spread into wide layouts. In Free mode your jellies ease to the new table size when you fold or unfold.

Accessibility and families

  • Every gate color also carries a shape symbol, so no puzzle depends on color alone.
  • Chill mode in Settings turns the level timer off. Easy and Normal levels never fail on time.
  • The game follows the system Reduce Motion setting.
  • Players under 13 send no analytics or tracking data, see no interstitial ads, cannot share photo clips, and only see rewarded ads they choose to watch, which are non-personalized.
  • Photo jellies are made on the device. The game uses the system photo picker and never uploads the photo.
