How can we help?

Answers to common questions, and a direct line to our small team.

Playing

How do squeeze gates work?

Drag a jelly block to the gate of its color. If the block fits, it plops out. A squeeze gate lets a block that is one cell wider than the gate squish through. Two cells too wide and it boings back, so plan which blocks go first.

A level feels too hard. Any tips?

Hard levels carry a red badge before you start. Clear the blocks that free the most space first, and save squeezes for the widest pieces. Boosters help on tricky boards: Time Jelly adds time, Extra Squeeze gives one more squeeze and Slap Out pops a stubborn block. Only Hard and Super Hard levels (and the Daily Jam) end when the clock runs out; on Easy and Normal levels the clock only decides your stars. Want no clock at all? Turn on Chill mode in Settings (every level then gives up to 2 stars), or play with your jellies in Free mode.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Levels, Free mode and your collection work without Wi-Fi. Ads and purchases need a connection.

How do I unlock new jellies?

Beat levels: every few levels the frost cracks and a new jelly friend pops out. There are 47 to collect in your Jelly Book. Jelly of the Day lets you try a locked one early.

Purchases and ads

I bought something but didn't get it

Open Settings > Restore Purchases. If a payment was still pending (for example waiting for Ask to Buy approval), the items arrive automatically once it's approved. Still missing? Use the form below and include the order number from your receipt email (Google orders start with "GPA.").

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you paid. iPhone / iPad: go to reportaproblem.apple.com. Android: open your Google Play order history, or write to us with your GPA order number and we'll help.

I got a new phone. Can I keep my progress?

"No Ads" and other one-time purchases restore with Settings > Restore Purchases when you use the same Apple or Google account. Level progress, Drops, boosters and your collection are saved on your device, not on our servers, so they move only if your phone's own backup and restore brings the app data along. We can't restore progress from our side because we don't keep a copy.

Can I remove ads?

Yes. The one-time No Ads purchase in the Shop removes interstitial ads for good. Rewarded videos stay, but they are always your choice. We never show banners, and we never show an interstitial right after you lose a level.

How do I turn off personalized ads?

In the game, turn on Settings > Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information. On iPhone and iPad you can also change tracking in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Google or Privacy settings.

Photos, privacy and families

Is my pet's photo uploaded anywhere?

No. Your photo is turned into a jelly on your device and stays there. It only leaves your phone if you choose to share a clip yourself. Deleting the game deletes your photo jellies. Details are in our privacy policy.

Is Squeeze Out! OK for kids?

The game is made for teens and adults. Younger players get kid-safe mode automatically: child-safe, non-personalized ads, no tracking, no photo sharing, and purchases need a grown-up. Parents can also use Apple Screen Time or Google Family Link to manage purchases and play time.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete my data. Deleting the game removes everything stored on your device; we can also delete the analytics and purchase records our partners hold.
