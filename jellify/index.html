Questions

Is my photo uploaded anywhere?

No. The photo is read by your browser, the background is removed by a small AI model that runs on your device (Google's MediaPipe MagicTouch, downloaded from this site the first time you jellify a photo), and the jelly is drawn on your screen. Nothing is sent to us or anyone else, and nothing about your image is logged.

Which photos work best?

One clear subject near the middle of the picture, not too small. If the wrong part of the photo turns into jelly, tap "Wrong part of the photo?" and tap the thing you want.

Which browsers work?

Recent Safari on iPhone and iPad, Chrome on Android, and Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a computer. Clips are saved as MP4 where the browser supports it (otherwise WebM). If your browser can't record video, you can save a picture instead.

Can I use the clip?

Yes, share it anywhere. Only use photos you have the right to use, and keep it kind.
