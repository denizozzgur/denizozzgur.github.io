Delete my data

Squeeze Out! (Space Runners Inc. on the App Store, Evercopy Inc. on Google Play) has no accounts, so most of your data lives only on your device.

Step 1: delete the game from your device

This removes everything stored on your device: level progress, Drops and boosters, your collection, settings, your age range, and all photo jellies and the photos used to make them.

  • iPhone / iPad: touch and hold the Squeeze Out! icon > Remove App > Delete App.
  • Android: touch and hold the icon > App info > Uninstall. To also clear any copy in your Google backup, open Settings > Google > Backup and manage app data.

One-time purchases such as No Ads stay linked to your Apple or Google account, so you can restore them if you reinstall. Consumable items (Drops, boosters) can't be restored after deletion.

Step 2 (optional): ask us to delete partner data

Our service providers keep some records tied to random identifiers, not to your name: gameplay analytics (PostHog), purchase records (RevenueCat) and install attribution (AppsFlyer). Email us and we will delete them, and ask our ad partners to do the same where they allow it.

Fastest way: in the game, open Settings > Privacy > Delete my data. It shows your Support ID (the random ID your records are stored under), starts a new ID on this device, and opens an email to us with the Support ID filled in. Delete the game afterwards if you also want your progress gone.

Without the game? Email a deletion request

We use the Support ID to find and delete the matching records at PostHog (analytics) and RevenueCat (purchases). Without it, a receipt's order number lets us find purchase records.

Please include:

  • the platform you play on (iPhone, iPad, Android or web);
  • roughly when you started playing;
  • if you made purchases, an order number from your Apple or Google receipt, so we can find those records.

Don't send passwords, card numbers or photos. We confirm your request within 10 business days and complete it within 45 days, as US state privacy laws require, or sooner where the law of your country sets a shorter deadline (see section 11 of the privacy policy). Records we must keep for tax and accounting law (such as purchase totals) are kept only for that purpose and deleted when the law allows.

Parents

If your child under 13 played Squeeze Out!, kid-safe mode limited what was collected to what the game needs to work. Email deniz@spacerunners.com and we will delete any records we can find and stop any further collection.

Stop data sharing without deleting

To keep playing but stop personalized ads and data sharing, turn on Settings > Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information in the game. See the privacy policy for details.
