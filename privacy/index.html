3. Your photos

  • When you turn a photo into a jelly, you pick the photo with your phone's system photo picker or take it with the camera. The game only sees the one photo you choose.
  • The cut-out and jelly effect run on your device. The photo and the jelly made from it are stored only in the game's storage on your device. We never upload, receive or keep your photos, and we don't create face templates or any biometric data from them.
  • If you tap Share on a clip that shows your photo, your phone's share sheet sends it to the app you choose. That sharing is between you and that app; the clip does not pass through our servers. Sharing photo clips is turned off in kid-safe mode.
  • Deleting the game from your device deletes your photos and photo jellies from the game.

4. Age screen and children

Squeeze Out! is a general-audience game for teens and adults and is not directed to children under 13. Because the game is cute and may still be played by children, the first time you play we ask for your month and year of birth with a neutral screen, before any ad, analytics or attribution service starts. We store only the resulting age range (under 13, 13–15, 16–17 or 18+) on your device, never the date.

Kid-safe mode (under 13):

  • Ads are child-directed, non-personalized and limited to general-audience (G) content, from Google AdMob only. No other ad network starts.
  • No install attribution, no advertising identifier, no tracking prompt and no data sent to Meta.
  • No analytics leave the device: gameplay statistics stay on the phone or tablet for that session only, and no analytics identifier is created or stored.
  • Photo clip sharing and invite links are turned off. Purchases require a grown-up to pass a parental gate, in addition to Apple's or Google's own parental controls.

This is how we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA): in kid-safe mode the game sends no analytics and no attribution data; the only third-party services it talks to are Google AdMob (child-directed, non-personalized ads) and, for purchases a grown-up approves, the app store and our purchase processor RevenueCat, which use a random purchase ID only to deliver and restore those purchases (support for the internal operations of the game). If you are a parent and believe your child gave us personal information, email deniz@spacerunners.com and we will delete it.

Teens 13–15: we do not sell or share their personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising unless they opt in (on iPhone or iPad, by choosing "Allow" in Apple's tracking prompt). Otherwise they see non-personalized ads only.

6. Your choices

  • Tracking on iPhone and iPad: the game asks once, with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change it any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. The game works the same either way.
  • Advertising ID on Android: you can reset or delete it in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Personalized ads and data sharing: use the in-game switch described in section 7.
  • No ads: the optional one-time "No Ads" purchase removes interstitial ads. Rewarded videos stay optional.
  • Notifications: the game asks before sending reminders, and you can turn them off in your device settings.
  • Deleting data: see denizozzgur.github.io/delete-data.

7. Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

We do not sell personal information for money. However, letting ad partners use identifiers and ad data to personalize ads, and sending purchase events to Meta to measure our ads, may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information, or as "targeted advertising", under California and other state laws.

You can opt out at any time:

  • In the game: Settings > Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information. When it is on, ads are non-personalized, ad partners are told to restrict data processing, and no purchase events go to Meta.
  • Global Privacy Control: if your browser or device sends a GPC signal, we treat it as an opt-out automatically.
  • By email: deniz@spacerunners.com with the subject "Do Not Sell or Share".

Kid-safe mode (under 13) and teens 13–15 who have not opted in are always opted out.

8. Your US state privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example California, Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah or Virginia), you may have the right to:

  • know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, and get a copy of it;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your information;
  • opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising;
  • appeal our decision if we decline your request (reply to our answer with the word "Appeal"; if we deny your appeal, you may contact your state Attorney General);
  • not be treated differently for using these rights. We never charge more or give a worse game for using them.

To make a request, email deniz@spacerunners.com or follow the steps here. Because we have no accounts, we may ask for details that help us find the data (such as the platform you play on, and for purchases the order number from your receipt). We answer within 45 days. 9. Retention, security and where data is processed

  • Game progress, settings, your age range and photo jellies stay on your device until you delete the game.
  • Analytics events: up to 24 months, then deleted or fully anonymized.
  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Support emails: up to 24 months after the conversation ends.
  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our partners process data in the United States and other countries under their own safeguards.
